# ParkSmart driver guide

## Finding parking

Drivers can search parking lots in ParkSmart. Search Parking shows each lot's location, current free spaces, total spaces, parking type, features, and hourly rate. Availability can change quickly, so check the app before booking. Prices are displayed in Bangladeshi taka (BDT), and times use Asia/Dhaka.

## Reservations

Sign in as a driver, add a vehicle in Profile, choose a parking lot, then select a future date, start time, end time, and vehicle. The start and end must be on the same date, and the end must be after the start. The selected vehicle must belong to the signed-in driver.

## Pricing and arrival

Each started hour is billed at the selected lot's hourly rate. For example, a stay of one hour and ten minutes is billed as two hours. At arrival, staff verify the reservation, vehicle, lot, assigned space, and booked time, then record entry. Drivers should use the reserved space unless staff direct them otherwise.

## Leaving and overstay

Ask parking staff to record the exit before leaving. Staff record the session duration and final amount and release the space. Leaving after the booked end time can increase the final amount and may create an overstay fine. Fines and their status appear in the driver's account.

## Cancellation and payments

Unpaid Pending or Confirmed bookings can be cancelled from My Reservations. Paid bookings require parking-operator handling for refunds or adjustments. Payment history appears in the driver's account. The current coursework application uses demo payments; it does not process real card or bank payments.

## Account information and support

Drivers manage reservations, vehicles, notifications, payment history, and fines in their account. The assistant cannot access or change private bookings. For immediate lot issues, contact on-site staff and provide the lot name and reservation details. For emergencies, follow posted site instructions and contact local emergency services.
