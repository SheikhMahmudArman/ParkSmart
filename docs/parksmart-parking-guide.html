How do I make a reservation?

  1. Create or sign in to a driver account. Public registration creates a driver account. Staff and administrator accounts are managed by ParkSmart administrators.
  2. Add your vehicle. Enter the vehicle plate number and, if useful, its make, model, and colour. The vehicle must belong to your account.
  3. Find a lot. Compare location, available spaces, parking type, features, and hourly rate.
  4. Choose the time. Select a future date and a start and end time on the same date. The end time must be after the start time.
  5. Review and submit. The estimated price is calculated from the selected interval and the lot’s hourly rate. Each started hour is billed.
Important: Availability shown in search is a snapshot. A reservation is only confirmed when ParkSmart successfully creates it and displays the reservation in your account.

Pricing and billing

QuestionParkSmart answer
What currency is used?Prices are shown in Bangladeshi Taka (BDT).
How is a booked price calculated?The parking rate is multiplied by the number of started hours in the booked interval. For example, a 1 hour 10 minute booking is billed as 2 hours.
Can the final amount change?Yes. Leaving after the booked end time can extend the billed period and may create an overstay fine. The final amount is recorded when staff complete the exit.
Do I pay twice for an overstay?No. The final parking amount includes the booked duration and any applicable extension. Review the payment record and fine details before paying.

Rates and lot features can differ by location. Always review the lot details shown at the time of booking.

Arrival, entry, and exit

Before you arrive

  • Check the lot location and your reservation date and times.
  • Bring the vehicle whose plate number is attached to the reservation.
  • Allow enough time to reach the entrance; entry outside the booked interval may be refused.

At the entrance

  • Show or provide your reservation details and vehicle plate number to staff.
  • Staff verify that the reservation is confirmed, current, and matched to the vehicle.
  • Do not occupy a different space unless staff direct you to do so.

When leaving

  • Ask staff to record your exit before driving away.
  • The system records the session duration and releases the space.
  • If you stayed beyond the booked time, check your account for the final amount or an overstay fine.

If something is wrong

  • Do not force entry or move barriers.
  • Speak with the on-site parking staff immediately.
  • Keep photos, timestamps, and your reservation number if you need to report a dispute.

Payments and receipts

ParkSmart keeps a payment history linked to your reservation and parking session. Payment records may show the amount, date, method, status, lot, and transaction reference.

Demonstration notice: The current ParkSmart application uses demo payments for coursework and testing. No real card or bank payment is processed by the demo payment flow. A production deployment should connect an approved payment provider before accepting real money.

When can I pay?

Depending on the configured workflow, payment is available after the parking session is completed. The amount payable is taken from the ParkSmart record rather than from a client-supplied amount.

Why do I see a fine?

Fines can be created for an overstay or another recorded parking violation. Review the reason and status in your Payment page. If you believe a charge is incorrect, contact the lot staff before making a duplicate payment.

Cancellations, changes, and expired bookings

  • Unpaid bookings in a cancellable status can be cancelled from your reservation list.
  • Paid bookings cannot be cancelled through the normal driver flow. A refund or adjustment must be handled by the parking operator.
  • A booking may be marked expired when its unused reservation window has passed.
  • If your plans change, cancel as early as possible so the space can return to availability.
  • ParkSmart does not promise that a cancelled space will remain available for later rebooking.

Accessibility, safety, and good parking practice

  • Use only the space assigned to your reservation and follow signs and staff instructions.
  • Keep drive lanes, pedestrian routes, emergency exits, ramps, and fire equipment clear.
  • Accessible, covered, EV, or other specialist spaces are subject to the facilities listed for the individual lot; confirm suitability with staff before arrival.
  • Do not leave valuables visible in your vehicle. Lock the vehicle and retain your keys.
  • In an emergency, follow the lot’s posted instructions and contact local emergency services. Parking staff can assist with site-related issues but are not emergency responders.

Account and privacy questions

What information is needed?

Your account uses your name and email. A phone number is optional. A vehicle plate number is required before you can reserve a space.

Can I manage more than one vehicle?

Yes. Add vehicles in Profile and select the correct vehicle when making a reservation. Remove a vehicle only when it has no active or future booking.

What are the user roles?

Drivers make reservations and view their own records. Staff manage reservations and record entry and exit. Administrators manage users, lots, spaces, payments, and reports.

Why did I receive an update?

Reservation status changes are recorded as notifications so you can follow the booking from creation through confirmation, entry, completion, cancellation, or expiry.

Need help?

For an immediate lot issue, speak to the on-site parking staff and provide your reservation number and vehicle plate. For an account or payment question, sign in and review the relevant reservation, notification, or payment record first.

When reporting a problem, include the lot name, date and time, vehicle plate, reservation number, and a short description of what happened. Never share your password.
