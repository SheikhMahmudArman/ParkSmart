Parking questions & answers

Find and reserve

Where can I see available parking?

Open Search Parking. Each lot lists its location, free and total spaces, type, features, and hourly rate. Availability can change, so check again before booking.

How do I reserve a space?

Sign in as a driver, add a vehicle in Profile, choose a lot, and select a future date, start time, and end time. The times must be on the same date, with the end after the start.

Why do I need to add a vehicle?

A vehicle attached to your account is required to book. Use that same vehicle when you arrive.

Price and payment

How is the parking price calculated?

Each started hour is charged at the lot’s hourly rate. For example, 1 hour 10 minutes is billed as 2 hours.

Can the final amount change?

Yes. Staying past the booked end time can increase the final amount and may result in an overstay fine.

Are payments real in this app?

No. The current coursework app uses demo payments; it does not process real card or bank payments.

At the parking lot

What should I bring when I arrive?

Bring the vehicle linked to your reservation and have your reservation details ready. Staff check the reservation, vehicle, lot, assigned space, and booked time.

Can I use a different parking space?

Use the space assigned to your reservation unless on-site staff direct you otherwise.

What should I do before leaving?

Ask staff to record your exit. Check your account for the final amount and any overstay fine.

Manage a booking

Can I cancel my reservation?

Unpaid Pending or Confirmed reservations can be cancelled from My Reservations. Paid reservations need operator handling for refunds or adjustments.

Where can I find my payments, fines, and reservations?

Sign in and open the relevant account section: My Reservations, Payment History, or Fines.

Can the assistant look up or change my booking?

No. The assistant cannot access or change private account information. Sign in to manage your booking.

Help

Who should I contact about a problem at a lot?

Contact on-site staff. Provide the lot name and reservation details. For an emergency, follow posted instructions and contact local emergency services.
